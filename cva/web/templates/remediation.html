What a re-scan of a remediated dataset means

A re-scan reports what was checked and what it found. If the re-scan raises no finding at severity low or above, that is a statement about the checks that ran on it — read together with that scan's own coverage statement, which will list everything it could not assess.

A detector plug-in may never retrain. Remediation is a separate, explicitly invoked component, and its output is re-assessed rather than trusted.
